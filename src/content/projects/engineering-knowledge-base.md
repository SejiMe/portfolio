## Business problem

Support teams need accessible, repeatable instructions for investigating application issues and handing work between engineers.

## Contribution

I developed an internal Astro engineering knowledge base at Central Luzon Doctors’ Hospital, documenting 20+ operational SOPs, API references, database repair scripts and hospital system workflows. SharePoint supported documentation management.

## Business value

The portal centralized technical knowledge used in troubleshooting and support handoffs. Its content connected engineering documentation with the clinical and administrative workflows those systems supported.

## Delivery context

This project was completed within my September 2023–July 2026 hospital role. Internal operational documents and repair scripts are omitted from this public case study.
