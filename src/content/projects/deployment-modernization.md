## Business problem

Manual releases and legacy hosting required repeated operational steps during application deployment.

## Contribution

I migrated hospital application hosting from Windows Server environments to Docker on Ubuntu Linux. I built SSH-based build and deployment scripts to automate release steps.

## Outcome

My general software resume reports deployment cutover improving from 30 minutes to under 2 seconds. This measures cutover time, rather than total build or infrastructure provisioning time.
